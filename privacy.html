Meditek Staff App – Privacy Policy

Meditek Staff App is an internal tool of Meditek Healthcare, Sivakasi, used only by its own staff to manage customer calls, collections and deliveries.

The app runs on Google Apps Script under the business owner's Google account. It reads and writes only the business's own Google Sheets, Drive files and Gmail messages needed for this work. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Staff data and customer data stay inside the business's Google account. Access to the app is limited to Meditek staff with a personal PIN.
